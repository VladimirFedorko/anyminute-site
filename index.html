Support

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Any Minute is a countdown alarm for the times you can’t miss. Name the time once, and it builds a curve of nudges to it — rare and quiet far out, frequent and loud up close.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, ideas: fedorko.studio@gmail.com.

Writing from inside the app — Settings → Contact support — fills in your app version, build and iOS version, and the error code if you write from an error screen. Nothing from your moments or history is attached, and you can edit the message before sending it.

A moment didn’t ring

  • Notifications. Settings → Any Minute → Notifications: allow them, and keep Time Sensitive Notifications on, so the nudges close to your time come through a Focus.
  • Alarms. The final ring of a moment uses iOS alarms. If you turned alarms off for Any Minute, the final ring becomes a regular time-sensitive notification, which Silent mode can mute.
  • Focus. A Focus that silences time-sensitive notifications silences Any Minute too.
  • When Any Minute knows a moment won’t fire, it says so on the moment itself — look for Won’t fire on Today.

Subscriptions

Every new download starts with 7 days of full access — no payment details, nothing to cancel. After that, Any Minute is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, sold by Apple. All three work with Family Sharing.

Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel it. Already paid but don’t see access? Settings → Restore purchases in the app brings it back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

If access ends, your moments stop ringing — Any Minute tells you before and on the day — but nothing is deleted: your moments and history stay, and come back the moment access does.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, or in Settings → your Apple Account → Purchase History.

Your data

There is no account and no server: your moments and history live on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on for your Apple Account, in your own private iCloud, so they follow you to a new iPhone. We cannot see them. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
